# EazyTech Solution - video ad script (27.7 s, 1080x1080)

Voice: on-device Kokoro voice "am_michael", 1.1x speed, normalised to about -16 LUFS. Silent-feed friendly: the big type and small caption lines carry the story without sound.

| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 s | Is your business running on loose pieces? | TOO MANY LOOSE PIECES? Four puzzle pieces tumble in: No website / Orders in a notebook / Ads that don't sell / Excel everywhere |
| 3.1 s | Orders in a notebook. Stock in Excel. Ads that never sell. None of it works together. | SOUND FAMILIAR? Each piece pulses as its problem is named. Then NONE OF IT FITS TOGETHER; two joins fail and bounce back |
| 8.85 s | At EazyTech Solution, we make it fit. | EAZYTECH SOLUTION, then WE MAKE IT FIT. |
| 11.4 s | A website that brings you enquiries. | Piece 1 snaps in and becomes Website & Apps. Caption: Websites that bring enquiries |
| 13.8 s | An online store that sells all day. | Piece 2 snaps in: Online Store |
| 16.15 s | Meta ads that bring real buyers. | Piece 3 snaps in: Meta Ads |
| 18.5 s | And software that runs your whole business. | Piece 4 snaps in: Business Software; the finished picture pulses |
| 21.15 s | We plan. We build. We grow your business. | WE PLAN. / WE BUILD. / WE GROW YOUR BUSINESS. on orange, teal, coral cuts |
| 25.0 s | Message us today for a free quote. | Logo rises, button MESSAGE US FOR A FREE QUOTE, eazytechsolution.com (held 2.1 s) |

Sound effects (free, no credit needed): short whoosh on every colour wipe and piece move, low hit on every snap and failed join, deep whoosh as the logo rises.
To change a line: edit the text in the table, regenerate its file with `npx hyperframes tts "<text>" -o assets/voice/lN.wav -v am_michael -s 1.1` (set HYPERFRAMES_PYTHON to .venv-tts\Scripts\python.exe), and adjust the start times in index.html.
